Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password. When you use the Service, we store the expense data you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to operate your account, save and display your expense data, and provide summaries and insights within the app. We do not use your financial data for advertising.

4. Data storage and security

Your data is stored in Spendly's database and protected with reasonable technical safeguards. Passwords are never stored in plain text. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal or financial data. We do not share your information with third parties, except where required by law or to operate core parts of the Service (such as hosting infrastructure).

6. Cookies and sessions

Spendly may use cookies or session identifiers to keep you signed in and remember your preferences. These are used only to operate the Service, not for third-party tracking or advertising.

7. Your choices

You can update your account details at any time from your profile. You may request deletion of your account and associated expense data by contacting us using the details below.

8. Children's privacy

Spendly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under that age.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Service after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact details provided on the Spendly website.
